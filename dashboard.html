Strategy & Guide

How the research engine is intended to work

PSX 20/80 V4 — Low Noise

A configurable technical screening framework, not a guarantee of performance.

  • Daily trend: Close above EMA20, EMA20 above EMA50, EMA50 above EMA100; EMA100 above EMA200.
  • Weekly confirmation: weekly close above weekly EMA20 and EMA50.
  • Momentum: RSI(14) between 55 and 70 and rising; MACD line above signal with positive/improving histogram.
  • Volume: relative volume at least 1.50 and mandatory volume-cross condition.
  • Risk controls: ADX(14) at least 20, extension cap 6%, ATR(14) stop multiplier 1.5, cooldown 5 bars.
  • Score weights: trend 30, momentum 20, volume 15, price action 20, weekly trend 15.

Score bands: Strong Buy ≥80 · Buy 70–79 · Wait 60–69 · No Trade below 60.

A valid score requires complete, adjusted daily and weekly price/volume history. Corporate actions, illiquidity and sudden news can invalidate technical setups.
